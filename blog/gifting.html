Jewellery Sellers Worth Trying (When You’re Done Scrolling Instagram)

At some point, jewellery shopping on Instagram just becomes noise. Everything looks good, everyone claims quality, and you’re left guessing what’s actually worth buying.

So instead of listing endless options, here are a few sellers that actually stand out — each for a different reason.

If you just want a safe, no-regret option: go with Kushal’s. Slightly higher priced, but you’ll understand why once you receive it.

Kushal's Fashion Jewellery

This is probably the easiest recommendation here. They cover everything — trendy office wear, 92.5 sterling silver, modern pieces, and even traditional designs.

Yes, the pricing is slightly higher than most Instagram sellers, but the quality backs it up. I’ve personally tried them, and it actually feels worth what you pay.

Zeraki Jewels

If you like getting value for money, this one stands out. They often run buy 1 get 1 offers and are especially known for bangles and mangalsutras.

They claim anti-tarnish quality — and from personal experience, the color actually holds up. So it’s not just marketing talk.

Mirana

This is where you go when you want something that stands out. The designs are more statement-heavy, slightly on the expensive side, but you can see why.

Also, a lot of influencers and celebrities have worn their pieces — so if you’re looking for something noticeable, this fits.

Sullie Jewels

More focused on traditional jewellery, especially antique-style pieces and necklaces. They also have a budget store, which is useful if you don’t want to spend too much.

I haven’t personally purchased from them yet, but visually, their collection looks solid and well-curated.

Daivik

Completely traditional in vibe. You’ll find both minimal and heavy jewellery — works well for weddings and festive occasions.

They sometimes recreate celebrity-inspired designs, and they actually look good. Pricing sits somewhere in the middle — not too high, not too cheap.
