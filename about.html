About SellerAtlas

SellerAtlas is a platform designed to help people discover trusted small businesses, independent creators, and unique brands across different online marketplaces.

Many talented sellers operate on platforms like Instagram, Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra, and independent websites, but discovering them can be difficult. SellerAtlas brings these sellers together in one place so buyers can explore reliable businesses more easily.

Our mission is simple: make it easier for people to find and support genuine small businesses and independent brands.

If you are a seller and would like to list your business, you can submit your store through the List Your Business section.
